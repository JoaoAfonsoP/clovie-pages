What's new

Everything that changed, version by version, newest first. The newest build is 1.0.6 (build 130). Written as each change was made, so nothing here is a guess.

1.0.6 · 15 August 2026

  • New: Report a problem, at the foot of Us under About. It opens your mail app with the version filled in, so what broke gets to us with the one fact we always need.
  • Fixed: A shared account the copying bug had already written over now puts itself right on the next update: the copied name and choices are cleared off the side they never belonged to, and cycle pages that choice was hiding come back on their own. Only the overwritten name has to be typed again, on the personal page, because the copy destroyed it everywhere.
  • Improved: If one of you deletes their account and somebody joins the shared account later, the newcomer now lands on the free side instead of on top of the person still there.
  • Fixed: On the phone that joined the shared account, opening the app could copy the other person's name and settings over your own, pull their workouts and meals under your side, and hide your cycle pages behind their choices, so the app greeted you both by the same name. A join that has already finished is now left alone.
  • Fixed: A row moves the moment you have held it, not half a second later. Putting a finger on a shopping item and setting off after a beat used to scroll the list instead of picking the row up, and said nothing about why. Every list in the app now lifts on the same shorter hold and buzzes as it does.
  • Improved: A past day is written the same way wherever it heads a row. Your weigh-ins and an exercise's history say "Yesterday" where the training list already did, instead of spelling the date out.
  • New: Sugar, salt and fibre are shown at last, on the day's food and on a recipe. The app had been asking for all three in the food form and reading them off every barcode, and then showing none of them. Salt is given to a tenth of a gram, the way the packet gives it.
  • New: Events and recipes can be swiped away like everything else. They were the last two lists where throwing something out meant opening it first. A repeating event still asks whether you mean this day or all of them.
  • New: A recipe says when you last cooked it, so you can see at a glance what you have had recently. The app had been writing that day down since the first version and never showing it.
  • New: The food day adds up protein, carbs and fat as well as calories, the same four numbers a recipe shows.
  • New: The period history says how heavy each one was. The three flow buttons were saving an answer that nothing ever read back.
  • Improved: Swiping a row away works the same on every list. The red behind it is the same width everywhere, and a delete that does not go through now brings the row back and says so instead of leaving you to wonder.
  • New: One day of a repeating event can be changed or taken off on its own. Deleting or saving one now asks whether you mean this day or all of them, and the form opens on the day you tapped instead of on the day the series started.
  • New: A training session tapped in the calendar opens. It used to say the session came from the training tab and leave you to go and find it. Yours opens where you can change it, the other person's opens to be read.
  • New: The shopping list has a plus button at the bottom right, where the gym, the calendar, the recipes, the scale and the food log all keep theirs. It carries the list back up to the box a line is written in and puts the cursor there.
  • New: Every line on the shopping list has a pencil. A letter in the wrong place used to mean throwing the line away and typing it again.
  • New: Clearing the bought things off the shopping list can be taken back. It says how many went and offers Undo for six seconds, and every row comes back ticked, under whoever ticked it.
  • New: A logged food can be put right. Tapping the row reopens the amount and the meal, instead of swiping it away and picking the food all over again.
  • New: A food saved to the household list can be corrected or removed; the way to it now sits beside the name. It was reachable only by knowing it was not there.
  • New: Which of you this phone is can be changed after setup, from the personal page. Tapping the wrong one during setup used to put every workout, meal and crossed-off item under the other person for good.
  • Improved: Every list in the app is reordered the same way now: hold a row, then move it. The little drag bars are gone from the shopping list and the recipe editor, where they were the only way and nothing said so.
  • Improved: A thing moves between aisles with a finger. Hold the row and drag it down past an aisle name; where it is let go decides both the aisle it joins and its place in it.
  • Improved: Ticking something off the shopping list leaves it on the list, crossed out, instead of taking it away.
  • Improved: Nothing hides behind holding a row any more. A routine is renamed from the pencil beside it, and a message is taken back with the same swipe that deletes a row everywhere else.
  • Improved: The custom exercise editor and the food editor ask before they let half-typed work go, the way the recipe, the routine and the event already did.
  • Improved: The recipe editor watches the whole form, not just the title, ingredients and steps. A description, the times, the number of people and the tags no longer leave without a word.
  • Improved: What is coming up on the home screen says what time it is, and a trip that leaves at six in the evening shows the hour under the days it covers.
  • Improved: A meal planned from a recipe, and an event copied off the phone's own calendar, arrive with the same reminder the form would have put on them.
  • Fixed: The gym says why a set was not written down. Add with the reps box empty used to do nothing at all and say nothing, and a weight that is not a number was saved as a set lifted with an empty bar.
  • Fixed: A whole-day event's reminder is read back the way it was picked, "Morning before" rather than "15 hours before".
  • Fixed: Searching for a food with no signal now says the food database could not be reached, instead of saying nothing was found. The barcode scanner says the same.

1.0.5 · 12 and 13 August 2026

  • New: A phone calendar can be shared whole. In "Phone calendars" on the Us screen each shown calendar carries a second switch that puts its events on the other phone, from thirty days back to four hundred ahead, and turning it off takes them back. Nothing is ever written into the phone's own calendar.
  • New: An event from the phone's own calendar can be copied to the shared calendar with one tap, "Put it on our calendar". Once the copy exists the button turns off, so a second tap cannot double the plan.
  • Improved: Every error message now follows the phone's language. On a Portuguese phone "Could not save" now reads "Não deu para guardar. Tenta outra vez.", and the rest of the error toasts made the same trip.
  • Improved: Back behaves the way Android promises. Back returns to Home first and only leaves the app from Home, and the edge swipe shows the screen behind before committing.
  • Improved: Ticking off a shopping item, saving a set, finishing a swipe-delete and a new record each give the small standard buzz, respecting the phone's own touch-feedback setting.
  • Improved: The striped testing block on the Us screen is gone; the example-data tools now exist only in development builds.
  • Fixed: An event added in Google Calendar shows up when you switch back to Clovie, instead of waiting for the app to be reopened.
  • Fixed: Turning the notification permission off no longer erases the scheduled reminders. They stay put, silent, and sound again once the permission is back.
  • Fixed: The "You usually buy" suggestions no longer learn from the example data; only what you tick off yourselves teaches the list.
  • Fixed: The food log left open across midnight wakes up on the new day instead of staying pinned to yesterday.
  • Fixed: A pairing code that went stale mends itself the moment the account screen shows it.
  • Fixed: At the biggest text sizes the cycle card wraps a fertile window that crosses into the next month instead of cutting it off.

1.0.4 · 7 and 8 August 2026

  • New: Your best is marked with a star, in your own colour: on the best set of an exercise, on the set that just beat it, on the day it came from, and on the session where it happened.
  • New: A new event comes with a reminder already on it, half an hour before, or nine the morning before for a whole-day event. Any event can still be given a different one, or none at all.
  • New: The Us screen has a Notifications section: event reminders, the two default times, the period reminder, and the Android permission button. Every switch there belongs to that phone alone.
  • New: Whoever tracks a cycle gets a quiet word at eight the evening before a period is expected, once three cycles have been logged. It is worked out on the phone itself, so nothing new leaves it.
  • New: Recipes can be filtered by how long they take, under twenty minutes or under forty-five, from the prep and cook times already on them.
  • New: A finger held on the cycle ring reads it out day by day.
  • New: Weights can be read in pounds. It is each phone's own setting, so one of you can read pounds while the other reads kilograms.
  • Improved: A set is written down the moment you save it, so backing out, a flat battery or Android closing the app costs nothing. Somebody once lost a whole session that way, which is what started all of this.
  • Improved: Starting a workout, correcting an old session, and building a routine, recipe or event are full pages now, and backing out with something half-written asks first.
  • Improved: Adding an exercise starts from the muscle groups with a search across all of them; each exercise has its own page that saves one set at a time, with history and graph beside it.
  • Improved: Any exercise can be renamed from a visible pencil, keeping its history and graph, and the new name shows on both phones.
  • Improved: Either of you can keep a cycle, or both, or neither. A partner's cycle is read-only from your side and never on your home screen.
  • Improved: The theme, accent, colours, language and confetti are each phone's own now; only the names, nicknames, colours and pronouns you set for each other are shared.
  • Improved: The licences list now includes Manrope, the app's font.
  • Fixed: A period stops saying "Happening now" once its days are over.
  • Fixed: Your calendar and reminders only ever use your own period, not a blend of both people's.
  • Fixed: The shopping list shows as many things as it used to; a taller row had pushed four of fourteen items off the bottom of the screen.
  • Fixed: A whole-day event's reminder row no longer overflows; the choices read "Morning before" and "Evening before".

1.0.3 · 6 August 2026

  • New: The weight, exercise and progress charts can be tapped or dragged to read the exact value and date behind any point.
  • Improved: An invite code now expires after a while, so an old code shared by accident cannot still be used much later.
  • Fixed: The days-together count no longer reads below zero when the start date sits in the future.
  • Fixed: Joining a household can no longer place both partners on the same side; it is caught and corrected automatically.
  • Fixed: Profiles and shared preferences sync properly between phones instead of one phone's change overwriting the other's.
  • Fixed: Two phones in different time zones can no longer disagree about which of two changes is newer.

1.0.1 · 28 July to 1 August 2026

  • New: The shopping list offers live suggestions while you type, ranked by your own past purchases, and grows a search box once it holds more than six items.
  • New: Log Food has a scan button that fills in the product from its barcode, and food search looks in Open Food Facts on top of your own saved foods.
  • New: A shopping item can be dragged within its aisle or moved to another aisle from a long press, and the order sticks.
  • New: A custom exercise can be created right from the picker, with its own muscle group and equipment; renaming it later keeps its history and records.
  • New: Ingredients, steps and exercises reorder by dragging their rows, the same way the shopping list already worked.
  • New: Each person can choose how they are referred to in the app: he/him, she/her or they/them.
  • New: Accounts can be deleted for good from Shared account under Us, and there are three new accent colours and a Sweet theme.
  • New: Sharing an invite sends a real link that opens Clovie on the other phone with the code already filled in.
  • New: There is a hidden game somewhere in the app, and July taught it sound, speed and manners.
  • Improved: The gym screen is five pages, Progress, Overview, Routines, History and Records, and swiping works from anywhere on the page.
  • Improved: Cycle tracking is personal to whoever logs it. A partner's cycle can be looked at, not changed, and whether you track one is asked once in onboarding and changeable from your own edit page.
  • Improved: Each side can only edit their own name; nicknames stay editable by either phone.
  • Improved: Colours, chips, row titles and tap-arrows line up consistently across Training, Kitchen, Today and the shopping list.
  • Improved: Cook mode uses the servings you scaled to, and "Start cooking" sits right below the servings control.
  • Improved: New messages show a mark until the thread is opened.
  • Improved: Exercise search finds a name with or without its accent, in either language.
  • Fixed: Adding an item already on the shopping list updates the existing line instead of printing it twice.
  • Fixed: The calendar shades an open period for as long as your own periods actually last, and catches up to today on its own after midnight.
  • Fixed: Day numbers at the largest text size no longer clip, in any calendar view.
  • Fixed: A period logged with a future date no longer crashes the edit dialog.
  • Fixed: A save interrupted by lost signal retries quietly in the background.
  • Fixed: Tightened the rules around who can make changes to the shared household. Nothing changes for normal use.
