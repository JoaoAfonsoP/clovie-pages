One app for two people.

A calendar you both write to, a shopping list either of you can add to, and a recipe book you keep together.

Try it early

Sign in together to share it, or use it on one phone.

Why we made this

We built Clovie for the two of us, to keep the training, the recipes and the shopping list in one place. We kept asking each other what we had on that week, so we put it in the app instead of asking.

One calendar for both of you

Each person has a colour, so you can tell whose event is whose without opening it. Whatever is already in the phone's own calendar shows up beside them, and Clovie only reads that calendar. It never writes anything back into it.

A shopping list on both phones

Add something from the sofa and it is already there when the other one is standing in the shop. Items sort themselves into aisles as you type, so the list comes out in the order you walk the store.

A recipe book you both keep

Save the ones you actually make. Tag them by meal or by mood, filter by how long they take, and send the ingredients of any of them straight to the shopping list.

A training log each

Pick a movement, type the weight and the reps, save. Each set is written down the moment you save it, so a flat battery halfway through a session costs you nothing. Every movement keeps its own history and its own line on a graph, and your bodyweight sits with them.

A cycle log, if you want one

Either of you can switch it on, during setup or from settings later. Your partner then sees it from their own side of the app, whether or not they keep one themselves, and all they can do is read it. It never turns up on their home screen.

Local by default

Without an account nothing leaves the phone. Sign in and what you write is stored in Europe, where only your two accounts can read it. Nothing is sold either way, and nothing watches how you use the app.

You can delete your account whenever you like, and whoever you are paired with keeps their copy exactly as it was. The full detail, including everything the app asks the phone for, is on the privacy page.

Get Clovie

Clovie is on Google Play in early access. Android only, for now.

Try it early
