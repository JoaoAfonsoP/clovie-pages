Try it early

Three taps, on the Android phone you want Clovie on.

  1. 1

    Join the tester group

    Google uses a group to know who is allowed in. On the page that opens, press Join group. If it says Ask to join, that is fine too, we let you in fast.

    Join the group
  2. 2

    Sign up as a tester

    On the page that opens, press Become a tester. Google then unlocks the app for your account.

    Become a tester
  3. 3

    Install from the Play Store

    The install link appears on that same page. Press Install.

Nothing showing yet? Google can take ten minutes. Open step 2 again.

We only ever see your email address. Privacy.
