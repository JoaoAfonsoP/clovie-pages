Be a tester

Google will not put Clovie on the Play Store until twelve people have been using it for a fortnight. That is the only thing standing in the way.

Testing means having Clovie on your phone and using it now and then. There is nothing to fill in, nothing to send back, and no meetings. The app arrives from the Play Store like any other and updates itself in the background from then on. Leave whenever you like.

How to join

  1. Tap the button below and join the group. Use the same Google account your phone's Play Store is signed in to — that is the one Google checks.
  2. You'll get one email from us with the Play Store link. Open it, tap Join, and install Clovie from the store.

Join the tester group

What we get to see

Your email address, because Google needs one to know you're allowed in. Nothing else. Nothing is ever posted to the group; it exists so Google can count you. What's inside the app stays as private as it is for anyone else, and that is written out on the privacy page.

If you already installed Clovie from this site

Write to hello@clovie.eu before you join. The download here and the one from the store are signed differently, so Android won't put one over the other, and there's an order to do it in that keeps what's on your phone.
