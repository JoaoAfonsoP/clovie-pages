Try it early

Do this on the phone you want Clovie on, signed in to Google. Three steps, about two minutes.

  1. Join the group. Press the first button, then press Join group. This is how Google knows who is allowed in.

    1. Join the group

  2. Tell Google you want to test. Press the second button, then press Become a tester. This is the step that counts: being in the group on its own gets you nothing.

    2. Become a tester

  3. Install it. A link to the Play Store appears on that same page. Open it and press Install, like any other app.

If the Play Store says the app is not available, wait ten minutes and open the second link again. Google takes a while to recognise a new tester.

That is everything. Clovie updates itself from then on, and you can leave whenever you like. Something not working? Write to us and we will sort it out.

What we get to see

Your email address, because Google needs one to know you are allowed in. Nothing else, and nothing is ever posted to the group. What is inside the app stays as private as it is for anyone else, and that is written out on the privacy page.
