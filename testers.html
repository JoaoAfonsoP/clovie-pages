Try it early

Clovie is arriving on Google Play, early access first. Getting in takes about two minutes.

Early access means having Clovie on your phone and using it as you like. There is nothing to fill in, nothing to send back, and no meetings. The app arrives from the Play Store like any other and updates itself in the background from then on. Leave whenever you like.

How to join

  1. Sign in to Google first, with the account your phone's Play Store uses. Google only offers the join button to someone signed in, and that account is the one it checks, so it has to be the same one.
  2. Tap the button and press Join group. The Play Store link comes by email. Open it on the phone, press Join, and install Clovie from the store like any other app.

What we get to see

Your email address, because Google needs one to know you're allowed in. Nothing else, and it is used for nothing else. Nothing is ever posted to the group. What's inside the app stays as private as it is for anyone else, and that is written out on the privacy page.
