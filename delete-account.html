Delete your account

Two ways to do it, depending on whether the app is still on your phone.

From the app

This is the faster option, and it takes effect immediately. It only appears once you've paired with someone, so if you're signed in alone, use the email option below instead.

  1. Open Clovie and go to Us.
  2. Tap the sync card to open Shared account, and sign in if you are not already.
  3. Near the bottom, tap Delete your account, then confirm.

This removes your sign-in itself. Nothing stays on your phone or in the shared space, and it cannot be undone. If someone is still paired with you, the calendar, list, recipes and everything else you both wrote stay exactly as they were for that person. Only your own seat and sign-in are removed.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, cannot sign in, or are signed in alone with nobody paired yet, email pjoao.vaz@gmail.com from the address your account uses, and ask for it to be deleted. Once that is confirmed, the same thing happens as above: your sign-in is removed, and anyone still paired with you keeps their copy of the shared space exactly as it was.

A copy you saved earlier

A copy you exported yourself is an ordinary file wherever you saved it. Deleting your account does not affect it. Removing that file, if you want it gone too, is a separate step.
