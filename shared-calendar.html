A calendar the two of you actually share

Plans go in once and both phones know.

Clovie keeps one calendar for the two of you. An event added on either phone shows on both, with the time it happens and a reminder that arrives when you chose, not fifteen hours early.

Each person has a colour, so a glance tells you whose evening it is. What already lives in the phone's own calendar shows up beside the shared things. Clovie only reads that calendar and never writes into it, so work stays work and yours stays yours.

Repeating plans behave like life. A weekly thing that is off one Tuesday is still a weekly thing, and one day of it can be changed or skipped on its own. A training session sits in the same week view, and tapping it opens it.
